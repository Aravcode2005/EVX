01 / Who we are

Learning that goes
out into the world.

“Evolving students into real-world innovators.”

EvolVIT bridges what gets taught in a classroom and what gets expected on a job floor. Through industrial visits, hackathons, seminars, and structured interview and placement preparation, we connect VIT Bhopal students with real industry problems.

We go beyond exposure. Our company partnerships place selected members in internships working on live products, real deadlines, and real user needs. Our mission is to translate textbook learning into industry-ready skill—one problem, one company, and one result at a time.

01 / Inaugural event

EvolART

A creative beginning.

Our first poster exhibition and competition marked EvolVIT’s launch as VIT Bhopal’s 100th club. Students explored human and artificial intelligence, digital detox, and student mental stress.

Event details & winners

81 teams entered online; the top 15 advanced to the Mini Auditorium. A five-member panel assessed relevance, creativity, detailing, and visual impact. The final exhibition took place in the presence of Vice Chancellor Dr. T. B. Shridharan, the DSW, and Assistant DSW.

02 / Online ideathon

Idea2Industry

Real problems. Practical ideas.

A multi-stage ideathon where students proposed technically feasible answers to real-world problems, developing teamwork, creativity, and an entrepreneurial mindset.

Event details & winners

Idea submission → shortlisting → live pitching → industrial visit selection. Judges evaluated innovation, originality, practicality, technical viability, scalability, and presentation clarity. The top 30 performers earned a place on the Appointy visit.

  • WinnerAditi Singh
  • 1st runner-upAnshima
  • 2nd runner-upShambhavi Dixit
03 / Industrial visit

Appointy, Bhopal

Inside the world of work.

40 students, including the top 30 Idea2Industry performers, visited Appointy to see how a Bhopal-based technology company builds scalable solutions for international clients.

Event details

Students explored planning, coding, testing, deployment, and continuous improvement; discussed AI, automation, data-driven decisions, QA, and user-centric design; and received career guidance through an open Q&A. Guided by Dr. Chandan Kumar Behera, Dr. Trapti Sharma, and Dr. Prakash N. B.

04 / With Defence Warrior Club

Youth as Game Changers for Viksit Bharat

A generation with purpose.

Themed “Role from 2026 to 2047,” this joint orientation explored students’ contribution to a Developed India by 2047.

Event details

Chief Guest Brigadier Rajneesh Gaur, Deputy Director General of NCC MP & CG Directorate, spoke about discipline, dedication, leadership, national service, and the NCC’s role in character building. Students discussed career, service, and citizenship. The event closed with feedback, a vote of thanks, and the National Anthem.

03 / Learn · Apply · Impact

The EvolVIT Internship Program.

Selected members work on meaningful live projects with industry professionals. Company collaborations, practical experience, expert feedback, technical and professional growth, and stronger career prospects sit at the heart of the program.

Datatrack, Bhopal / 3 months

Learn by building real products.

Hands-on work in n8n workflow automation, 3D web development, and modern build frameworks such as Speckit, applied across four live projects.

  • Uprole — uprole.me
  • AI Worksheet Analyzer — pracup.co.in
  • Atithi — Visitor Management System (internal build)
  • Centralized EV Discovery Platform (internal build)

The club record reports this internship was recognised for 4 college credits by the respective authority.

Quantumard Software Development / 1 month

A focused full-stack build sprint.

A Project Management System for EvolVIT’s exhibit workflows, with role-based access for administrators, managers, and members.

  • Project and task tracking
  • Admin and student profile management
  • Department controls through an admin panel
  • JWT-based secure, role-based authentication

Built with Next.js, Tailwind, TypeScript, Node, Express, and MongoDB.

The path to the Research Team

The Research Team builds industry partnerships. Top-performing club members progress through a five-stage evaluation to qualify for internship opportunities.

Initial screening

Applications are reviewed to shortlist suitable candidates.

Skill assessment

Individual mini-projects test technical competence within a defined timeline.

Performance evaluation

Candidates are evaluated on their assigned mini-projects.

Company assessment

The hiring company conducts an additional assessment or test.

Internship placement

Successful candidates move into the internship.
